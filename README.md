# SWTCR

**Your apps, one edge away.**

SWTCR is an alternative to Stage Manager for people who want quick access to a custom selection of apps. It lives at the **right edge of your screen**: choose your apps, hover at the edge, and switch to an app or one of its windows.

A native macOS menu-bar utility, with no Dock icon.

## Features

- Choose up to **7 apps** and arrange them in your preferred order.
- Hover at the right screen edge to reveal a vertical panel with large app icons.
- Click an app to activate it, or launch it if it is closed.
- Hover over an icon to browse window titles and preview images.
- Click a window to bring it forward, including restoring minimized windows.
- Keep the app icons in a fixed position while previews appear beside them.
- On multiple displays, use the right edge of the display containing your pointer. Crossing to another display cancels a pending activation.

The panel opens after about **250 ms** at the edge and closes about **300 ms** after leaving it. Choosing an app or window closes the panel immediately.

## Installation

1. Download and open the SWTCR DMG.
2. Drag **SWTCR** into **Applications**.
3. Launch SWTCR from Applications and eject the disk image.
4. In Settings, select **Add Application…** to choose your apps.
5. Use the up/down controls to set their order.

Run the installed copy from Applications when granting permissions.

## Permissions

SWTCR can launch and switch apps without either permission below.

| Permission | Enables |
| --- | --- |
| **Accessibility** | Reading window titles and selecting or restoring individual windows. |
| **Screen Recording** | Small preview images of the windows you browse. Accessibility is also needed to discover those windows. |

Use the **Enable** buttons in SWTCR Settings. macOS manages these permissions under **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. The screen capture permission's name can vary by macOS version.

If preview images do not appear after granting Screen Recording, quit SWTCR from its menu-bar menu and reopen it.

## How to use

Hold your pointer at the right screen edge until the app panel appears. Hover over an icon to browse windows, then click an icon or window to switch.

The panel stays open while the pointer is over it, its previews, or the triggering edge. After selecting an app or window, move away from the edge before triggering it again.

Open **Settings…** or **Quit SWTCR** through the menu-bar icon.

## Privacy

Selected apps and their order are stored locally on your Mac. The current app has no account system or analytics, and does not upload window titles or preview images.

Previews are snapshots requested when browsing an app, not a continuous recording. Images are held in memory, discarded on dismissal or when browsing another app, and are not saved as image files.

## Current limitations

- Some minimized, protected, or unavailable windows show a title instead of a preview.
- Windows on other Spaces are included where macOS exposes them. Behaviour varies by app and fullscreen settings.
- Up to 12 windows receive preview images per app hover; other discovered windows retain selectable titles.
- Launch at Login is not included yet.
- Multi-display seams, fullscreen Spaces, and interaction with Stage Manager need broader testing. Resource usage has not yet been measured systematically.

## Troubleshooting

- **No icons:** add apps in SWTCR Settings.
- **No panel:** confirm SWTCR is running in the menu bar and hold the pointer at the right edge without crossing to another display.
- **No window titles or selection:** check Accessibility permission for the installed app.
- **Titles but no images:** check Screen Recording permission and relaunch. Some windows cannot provide a preview.

To uninstall, quit SWTCR and move it from Applications to the Trash. You can also remove its entries from the macOS privacy permission lists. Local preferences may remain.
